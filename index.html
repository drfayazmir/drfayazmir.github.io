About

I came to sleep research through the brainstem, and I have not really left it.

My doctoral work at Jawaharlal Nehru University, with Professor Sushil K. Jha, asked a narrow question that turned out to open onto a much wider one: how does the brain's chemosensory machinery decide when REM sleep is permitted? Working in the locus coeruleus and the nucleus tractus solitarius, I found that acid-sensing ion channels and inwardly rectifying potassium channels do more than report CO₂ — they gate the NREM-to-REM transition itself. State regulation and metabolic sensing are not parallel systems. They are the same system, read at different timescales.

That conclusion is what moved me toward bioenergetics. At MGH, in Dr. Christa Nehs's laboratory, I record extracellular ATP with GRAB-ATP1.0 alongside chronic EEG/EMG polysomnography in freely behaving mice, which lets me ask what the energetic cost of each vigilance state actually is and how that cost shifts with age, with ketogenic diet, and in amyloid-bearing models. The working hypothesis — ATP dynamics, sleep architecture, and glymphatic clearance as one coupled loop — is still a hypothesis, and parts of it will probably be wrong. The parts I can test, I am testing.

Most of what I do is building: surgeries, viral vectors, fiber placements, and the MATLAB pipelines that turn a night of photometry and EEG into scored, aligned, analyzable signal. I have spent as much time debugging artifact rejection as designing experiments, and I have stopped treating that as a distraction from the science.

My training is entirely in rodents. I am clear-eyed about what that means for translation, and I think the honest version of the work is more useful than the inflated one.

What I bring to the rig

In vivo

Chronic polysomnography

EEG/EMG headmounts, multi-day recordings in freely behaving mice and rats, and sleep scoring pipelines validated against manual epochs.

Photometry

Fiber photometry

GRAB-ATP1.0 and GCaMP6 recordings time-locked to vigilance state, including dual-site configurations and motion-corrected ΔF/F extraction.

Circuits

Causal manipulation

Stereotaxic surgery, AAV vector delivery, DREADD-based chemogenetics, and targeted microinjection into brainstem nuclei.

Imaging

Two-photon microscopy

Longitudinal in vivo imaging for cellular and vascular readouts in disease models, paired with histological validation.

Analysis

Signal pipelines

MATLAB, Python, and R workflows for spectral analysis, state classification, event detection, and photometry–EEG alignment from TDT systems.

Models

Aging & Alzheimer's

Amyloid-bearing and aged cohorts, dietary interventions, and sleep-deprivation paradigms designed around the constraints of each model.

Scientific Reports · 2024

Kir channels in the NTS gate REM sleep

Barium blockade of inwardly rectifying potassium channels in the nucleus tractus solitarius reduced wakefulness and shifted REM output, placing a chemosensory relay directly inside the REM executive network.

Neurosci Bull · 2021

LC acid-sensing ion channels and state transition

ASICs in the locus coeruleus modulate sleep–wakefulness and the NREM-to-REM transition, suggesting that a mild CO₂ rise during NREM is one of the signals permitting entry into REM.
